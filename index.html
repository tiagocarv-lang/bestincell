Planner apps for ADHD brains and busy families

BestInCell apps are digital planners that open like real apps on your phone, tablet or computer. Each one shows one small next step at a time, with a 5-minute focus timer, a "Stuck?" helper and a brain dump, so planning never feels overwhelming.

Is this a printable or a real app?

A real app. It runs in your browser, you can add it to your home screen, and it works offline.

Is there a monthly subscription?

No. You pay once on Etsy and keep the app forever. No account, no ads.

Where is my data saved?

Only on your device. Nothing is sent to a server. You can export a backup any time.

Which devices work?

iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac, Windows and Chromebook.
